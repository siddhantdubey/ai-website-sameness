Questions

Before you ask.

Can we bring our existing projects with us?

Yes. Keel imports from Trello, Asana, Jira, and Linear, or from a CSV file. Assignees, due dates, and comments come along.

What happens when the free plan runs out of room?

Nothing gets locked or deleted. You can keep working in your existing projects, and you'll need to upgrade or archive one to start another.

Do clients and contractors count as paid seats?

No. Guests can view and comment on the projects you share with them at no charge on the Team and Business plans.

Can we cancel partway through a year?

Yes. Cancel any time and we refund the unused months. You can export everything to CSV or JSON before you go.
