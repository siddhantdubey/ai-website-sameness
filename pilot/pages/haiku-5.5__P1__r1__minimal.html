FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. The Starter plan is free forever for teams of up to five people, with unlimited projects and core task views.

Can I import my existing projects?

Absolutely. Taskflow supports CSV imports and direct migrations from popular project management tools.

How does the free trial work?

Pro comes with a 14-day free trial with full access to every feature. No credit card is required to start.

Is my data secure?

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We're SOC 2 Type II compliant, and you can export your data at any time.
