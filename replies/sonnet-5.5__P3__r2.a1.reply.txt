The portfolio is written to `portfolio.html`, a single file of about 20 KB with no external dependencies. I haven't opened it in a browser, so the layout and interactions are untested.

**What's in it**
- **Hero:** large serif headline, a rotated "print" frame, and a booking-status line.
- **Work:** a masonry gallery with category filters (Portraits, Weddings, Editorial, Travel). Clicking a photo opens a lightbox with arrow-key, button and click-outside controls.
- **About:** a dark section with a short bio, stats and a client list.
- **Services:** three pricing cards.
- **Testimonial and contact:** the form opens the visitor's mail client with the message pre-filled, so it needs no backend.
- **Responsiveness:** a mobile menu, `prefers-reduced-motion` support, keyboard focus states and aria labels.

**Placeholders to replace**
- **Name and copy:** "Elena Varga", Lisbon, the clients, prices and testimonial are all invented.
- **Email:** `hello@example.com` appears in the contact section and in the form's mailto link. The social links point to `#`.
- **Photos:** the gallery shows generated colour studies. To use real images, add `src:"photos/your-file.jpg"` to an entry in the `PHOTOS` array at the top of the script. Set `r` to the image's width/height ratio so the grid keeps its layout. If a file fails to load, the placeholder shows instead.

Since you asked for one self-contained file, real photos will sit beside it in a folder. To make it truly standalone, embed them as base64 `src` values.
