  <section id="how">
    <div class="wrap split">
      <div class="head">
        <span class="mono">How it works</span>
        <h2>Four opinions, held firmly.</h2>
      </div>
      <div>
        <div class="item">
          <span class="n">01</span>
          <div>
            <h3>Blocked is a state, not a comment.</h3>
            <p>Mark a task blocked and you have to say on whom or on what. It floats to the top of the project, shows how many days it’s been stuck, and pings the person holding it up, once, not daily.</p>
          </div>
        </div>
        <div class="item">
          <span class="n">02</span>
          <div>
            <h3>One owner. One date.</h3>
            <p>A task can have collaborators, but only one person answers for it. Moving a date asks for a reason in a single line, and that line shows up in the history. Nobody gets blamed; the plan just stays honest.</p>
          </div>
        </div>
        <div class="item">
          <span class="n">03</span>
          <div>
            <h3>A Friday digest you don’t have to write.</h3>
            <p>Every Friday at 3pm, Plumb assembles what shipped, what slipped and what’s blocked into a plain-text note you can forward as-is. It’s built from the task history, so it can’t say anything the tasks don’t.</p>
          </div>
        </div>
        <div class="item">
          <span class="n">04</span>
          <div>
            <h3>Keyboard first, mouse fine.</h3>
            <p>Press <code>c</code> to create, <code>b</code> to block, <code>/</code> to jump anywhere. Every list is a URL you can paste into chat, and it opens in under a second on a bad hotel connection.</p>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>
  </section>
