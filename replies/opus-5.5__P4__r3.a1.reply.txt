I've written the page to `index.html`. It's one HTML file with no external fonts, images or scripts, so it works offline.

**The firm:** "Hollis & Achterberg" is a made-up two-partner practice covering estate planning, small business and real estate. Its warm, trustworthy look uses a paper-toned background, dark green, and serif headings.

**Sections:**
- **Sticky header** with the firm's monogram, navigation and a "Book a consultation" button. On phones the links collapse into a menu.
- **Hero:** the main headline, a short description, two buttons, and an "At a glance" box with response time, the free consult and office hours.
- **Practice areas:** three columns, each listing services with starting prices.
- **How we work:** four numbered steps on a green band.
- **Attorneys:** two bios with background and credentials. The photos are monogram placeholders.
- **Testimonials:** quotes with the usual "past results don't guarantee outcomes" note.
- **FAQ:** questions that open and close, built without JavaScript.
- **Contact:** office details and a consultation form. The form checks entries and requires the visitor to confirm that submitting it doesn't create an attorney–client relationship.
- **Footer** with the attorney-advertising disclaimer.

**Before using it for a real firm:**
- Replace the firm details, which are all invented: names, bios, prices, phone, address and email. The phone, address and email are placeholders.
- Swap the monogram placeholders for real headshots.
- Connect the form to an email or form service. Right now it only shows a thank-you message.
- Check the disclaimer wording against your state bar's advertising rules.
