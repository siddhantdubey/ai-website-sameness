I've built the landing page as a single file, **`index.html`**, with no external dependencies (no fonts, images or CDNs). I haven't opened it in a browser yet.

**The concept:** "Plotline," a project management app. It has a warm editorial look: off-white background, near-black text, an orange-red accent, serif headlines, and hard offset shadows on buttons and cards.

**Sections:**
1. **Sticky nav** that collapses into a menu on mobile.
2. **Hero** with a demo board you can drag cards around on. Column counts update when a card moves, and a short "team notified" message appears.
3. **Customer logo strip** (text-only wordmarks).
4. **Problem and stats.** The numbers count up when you scroll to them.
5. **Product tour** with three tabs: a timeline with a "today" marker, async status updates with On track / At risk labels, and a workload view whose bars animate in and flag an overloaded teammate.
6. **How it works:** three steps.
7. **Testimonial.**
8. **Pricing** with a monthly/yearly switch that updates the prices (20% off yearly).
9. **FAQ** using expandable questions.
10. **Signup form** that shows a success message when submitted.
11. **Footer.**

**Other details:**
- Layout adapts at 960px and 620px widths.
- Visible keyboard focus outlines and labelled controls.
- Animations turn off for people who have reduced motion enabled.
- Sections fade in as you scroll.

All brand names, customers, testimonials and stats are placeholders, so swap in real content before using it.
