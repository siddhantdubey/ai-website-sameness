      <div class="sec-head">
        <span class="num">01 &mdash; How it works</span>
        <h2>A week is the right size for a plan.</h2>
      </div>
      <div class="points">
        <p class="side-note">Most tools let you plan forever. Docket asks you to commit to five days, then gets out of the way.</p>
        <ol>
          <li>
            <h4>Monday: pull in what matters</h4>
            <p>Drag work from &ldquo;Later&rdquo; into this week. Sort it into must, should, and if-there&rsquo;s-time. That&rsquo;s the whole planning meeting.</p>
          </li>
          <li>
            <h4>All week: one list, one owner per line</h4>
            <p>Every task has exactly one person&rsquo;s initials on it. Shared ownership is how things quietly don&rsquo;t happen.</p>
          </li>
          <li>
            <h4>Blocked means blocked</h4>
            <p>Mark a task as waiting and say on whom. Docket nudges that person once &mdash; not hourly &mdash; and shows it in red until it moves.</p>
          </li>
          <li>
            <h4>Friday: roll over or let go</h4>
            <p>Unfinished work doesn&rsquo;t silently pile up. You decide, line by line, whether it carries into next week or goes back to Later.</p>
          </li>
        </ol>
      </div>

      <div class="not-list">
        <span></span>
        <div>
          <h5>Things we left out on purpose</h5>
          <p><s>Story points</s> &mdash; you&rsquo;re guessing either way.</p>
          <p><s>Gantt charts</s> &mdash; export a CSV if a client insists.</p>
          <p><s>Seventeen custom statuses</s> &mdash; open, waiting, review, done.</p>
          <p><s>An AI that writes your tasks</s> &mdash; you know what needs doing.</p>
        </div>
      </div>
