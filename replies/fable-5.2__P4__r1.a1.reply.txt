I wrote the landing page to `index.html` in the working directory: one file, no external fonts, images or scripts. I have not opened it in a browser, so the layout is untested.

The firm is an invented one, "Ibarra & Lowe", a three-attorney practice in Duluth handling estates, probate, real estate and small business. The page runs in this order:

- **Hero:** the headline is set beside a yellow legal pad that spells out what happens when you call.
- **Practice areas:** four areas, each with the kinds of matters it covers.
- **Fees:** a flat-fee schedule with prices listed.
- **Attorneys:** three short bios, text only, with no photos.
- **Client quote:** a single testimonial.
- **FAQ:** four questions that expand on click.
- **Contact:** office details and a form.

Before this goes live:

- **Placeholder content:** the firm name, attorneys, bios, prices, address, phone number, founding year and the client quote are all invented. The email uses a `.example` domain.
- **Contact form:** there is no backend. Submitting opens the visitor's email app with the message pre-filled, addressed to the `FIRM_EMAIL` constant near the bottom of the file. If you'd rather it post to a form service or your own endpoint, swap out the submit handler.
- **Legal wording:** the footer carries a generic "attorney advertising" disclaimer. Bar advertising rules vary by state, especially for testimonials and stated fees, so check that and the client quote against your jurisdiction's rules.
