The landing page is at `index.html` in the working directory: one file, no external fonts, scripts or images. I syntax-checked the script but have not opened the page in a browser, so the layout is unverified visually.

The product is a fictional app called **Reckon**, pitched on a single idea: it calculates the real ship date and tells you when it moves.

- **Hero demo:** a working mini project plan sits under the headline. Four "what if" switches (a review runs long, scope is added, a vendor slips, a QA engineer is borrowed) reschedule the dependent tasks. The forecast date, the margin against the committed date and a plain-language note on who gets notified all update.
- **Rest of the page:** three differentiators with small product specimens, a short list of standard features, one customer quote, three pricing tiers and a closing sign-up band.
- **Responsive and accessible:** it reflows down to phone width, the switches are keyboard-operable, and it respects reduced-motion settings.

Things to replace before this goes anywhere real:

- **Placeholder content:** the name, copy, customer quote, prices and integration list are all invented.
- **Sign-up form:** it isn't connected to anything; submitting just shows a note saying so.
- **Dead links:** Changelog, Sign in, "Talk to us" and the footer legal links point to `#`.
- **Demo dates:** the plan is hardcoded around today (Nov 23, 2026), so the "today" marker will go stale.
