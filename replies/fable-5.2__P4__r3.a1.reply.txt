The page is for a fictional three-lawyer firm, Bramhall & Reyes in Providence. The design borrows from a marked-up contract: the headline strikes out a piece of legalese and highlights the plain-English replacement, with the partners' comments in the margin. I haven't opened it in a browser, so check it before using it.

- **Placeholder content:** the firm name, lawyers, address, phone number, fees and the client quote are all invented. Swap in the real details before publishing, and check the footer disclaimer against your state bar's advertising rules.
- **Contact form:** there is no backend, so submitting opens the visitor's email app with the message pre-filled, addressed to a placeholder address. Point it at a real inbox or a form service when you have one.
- **Fonts:** it uses system fonts only, so the file makes no external requests, but the serif will look slightly different on Mac, Windows and Linux.

Sections run in this order: hero, practice areas, how an engagement works, a published fee schedule, the three lawyers, a client quote, and contact.
