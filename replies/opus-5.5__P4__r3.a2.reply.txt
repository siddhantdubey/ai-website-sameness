I've built the landing page as a single self-contained HTML file: `index.html`. It uses no external fonts, images or scripts, so it works offline.

**What's on the page**, for a made-up three-attorney firm, Marsh & Okafor:
- **Header** that stays at the top, with phone number, hours, and a menu that collapses on phones.
- **Hero section** with the headline, two buttons, a client quote and three firm figures.
- **Practice areas:** estate planning, real estate, small business and family law, each with sub-services.
- **How we work:** four steps from consultation to wrap-up, plus three sample flat fees.
- **Attorney profiles** with initials in place of photos, ready to swap for real headshots.
- **Testimonials** that rotate automatically, with buttons to pick one and the usual "results vary" disclaimer.
- **FAQ** that expands and collapses without JavaScript.
- **Contact form** that checks entries in the browser and shows a thank-you message. It includes the "this doesn't make me your lawyer" checkbox.
- **Footer** with an attorney advertising disclaimer.

**Design:** off-white paper background, deep green, brass and dark red accents, and classic serif headings. It adapts to phone, tablet and desktop. I also included keyboard and screen-reader support, and it respects the reduced-motion setting.

**Checks:** I ran checks on the HTML, CSS and JavaScript structure and on the internal links and form labels. But the output of those commands didn't come back to me, so I can't confirm they passed. I also haven't viewed the page in a browser. Separately, I estimated that the small brass labels didn't have enough contrast against the background, so I darkened them (still brass-colored) to meet the usual accessibility standard. Opening the file in a browser is the real test.

**Before using it for real:**
- The form only works in the browser and doesn't send anything yet. Connect it to Formspree, Netlify Forms or your own server.
- The firm name, people, address, prices, figures and testimonials are all placeholders.
- Advertising rules for lawyers vary by state. Have the testimonials and disclaimers checked against your state bar's rules.
