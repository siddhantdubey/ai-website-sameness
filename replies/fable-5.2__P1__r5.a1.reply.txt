I wrote the landing page to `index.html` in the working directory; I have not opened it in a browser, so the layout and animations are unchecked.

The page is for a fictional app called Plumb, pitched on honest delivery forecasts ("Know the real date"). It runs from a hero with a product mock through how it works, features, a testimonial, pricing and an FAQ to a sign-up section. The file makes no external requests: no web fonts, images or scripts.

Three things are interactive:
- **Product mock:** switches between Timeline, Board and List views.
- **Pricing:** a toggle switches between monthly and annual prices.
- **Sign-up form:** it validates the email and shows a confirmation, but it is not connected to anything and sends nothing.

The product name, company names, testimonial, statistics and prices are all invented placeholders, and the nav and footer links mostly point to `#`. Replace them before publishing.
