I created **`index.html`**, a single self-contained file with no external fonts, images, or scripts. The HTML structure checks out clean. I couldn't run a syntax check on the JavaScript because Node isn't installed here, so I reviewed it by hand. Open the file in a browser to try it.

**The concept:** a made-up app called **Cadence**, a project management tool built around weekly cycles. The design looks like a printed page: warm off-white background, serif headlines, dark ink text, a red-orange accent color, and solid offset shadows. I chose this to avoid the standard gradient look most SaaS sites use.

**Sections**
- **Sticky nav** with an animated logo and a menu button on small screens.
- **Hero** with a headline that gets struck through as the page loads. Next to it is a **working kanban board**: you can drag cards between columns, and the column counts and the "% shipped" bar update. On touch screens, double-tap a card to move it to the next column.
- **Customer name row**, then a dark **problem section** with numbers that count up as you scroll to them.
- **Features** in a grid of different-sized cards, including an animated throughput chart, keyboard shortcut keys, and a sample status digest.
- **How it works**: three steps, Monday to Friday.
- **Testimonials**: one large quote and three short ones.
- **Pricing**: three plans, with a working monthly/yearly switch that changes the prices.
- **FAQ**: questions that expand and collapse.
- **Sign-up section** with an email field that checks the address format, then the footer.

**Details:** content fades in as you scroll; the layout adapts to tablets and phones; animation is turned off if the visitor has reduced motion enabled; keyboard focus is clearly visible; the buttons are proper accessible controls.

The name "Cadence", the customer names, the people, and all the numbers are placeholders. Replace them with your own.
