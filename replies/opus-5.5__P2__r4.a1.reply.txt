I've built the landing page as a single self-contained HTML file at `index.html`. It has no external fonts, images or scripts; all the artwork is inline SVG.

I haven't opened it in a browser. The checks I ran printed no output, so I can't confirm it parses or that the JavaScript runs cleanly.

The restaurant is fictional: **Osteria Lucciola** ("firefly"), a 32-seat family place that has been on the same corner since 1998. Everything on the page is placeholder content: the name, the address in Philadelphia, the `555` phone number, the prices and the quotes.

**What's on the page**
- **Open/closed bar** at the top that uses the visitor's clock, e.g. "Open now · kitchen until 10pm" or "Closed · opens tomorrow at 5pm".
- **Opening section** with a drawn plate of pasta on a checked tablecloth, a spinning "Fatto a mano" stamp and a taped-on note.
- **Scrolling strip** of signature dishes.
- **Our Story**, with a drawn storefront, the family history and a few quick facts.
- **Menu** with four tabs (Antipasti, Primi, Secondi, Dolci). The tabs work with the arrow keys and dishes are marked vegetarian or "House" where relevant.
- **Chalkboard of tonight's specials**, which changes by day of the week. On Mondays it says the place is closed.
- **Three customer quotes.**
- **Visit section** with the address, a small drawn street map and a week of hours with today highlighted.
- **Reservation form**:
  - Times are offered only for the chosen day's open hours, and past times are hidden if the date is today.
  - Mondays are blocked.
  - Name and phone are required.
  - Submitting shows a personalized confirmation.
- **Footer** with a newsletter sign-up.

The design uses warm cream, tomato red, olive and gold, classic serif fonts and a faint paper texture. It works on phones (with a collapsing menu), respects the reduced-motion setting and uses accessible tab and radio-button patterns.

**Before using it for real**
- The reservation form and newsletter sign-up don't send anything anywhere; they only show a confirmation on the page. They need to be connected to a booking service and an email tool.
- To change opening hours or the daily specials, edit the `HOURS` and `SPECIALS` lists at the top of the script near the bottom of the file.
