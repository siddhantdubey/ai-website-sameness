The page is for an invented photographer, "Odile Varga" in Rotterdam, who shoots buildings, land and objects. It has:

- **Hero**: a headline, a short introduction and one wide featured frame.
- **Selected work**: 12 frames you can filter by subject; each opens in a full-screen viewer with arrow-key navigation.
- **About**: a short biography and a facts list.
- **Commissions**: three services with starting rates and a four-step process.
- **Contact**: a large email link, a copy-address button and the next available booking date.

It follows the system light or dark setting and adapts to phone widths.
