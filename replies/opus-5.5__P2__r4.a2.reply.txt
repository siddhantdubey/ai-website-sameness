I built the landing page as a single file: `index.html` (about 38 KB). It loads no external fonts, images or scripts; the illustrations are inline SVG. I checked that the HTML parses, but I haven't opened it in a browser yet.

**The concept:** *Lucciola* ("firefly") is a made-up 28-seat family trattoria at 418 Alder Street. It's styled like a printed menu on warm cream paper, with tomato red and olive accents.

**Sections**
- **Awning and header:** a red-and-cream striped awning runs across the top. The nav shows a live "Open · until 10 pm" or "Closed · opens tomorrow 5 pm" indicator that updates from the visitor's clock.
- **Hero:** a large serif headline, a hand-drawn plate of tagliatelle, and a rotated "€0 corkage on Tuesdays" stamp.
- **Scrolling ticker:** short house notes that move across the page. It stops if the visitor has reduced motion turned on.
- **Tonight:** the family's story next to a chalkboard of tonight's specials, dated today in Italian.
- **Menu:** tabs for Antipasti, Pasta, Secondi and Dolci. Prices are lined up with dotted leaders, and dishes are tagged House or V (vegetarian). The tabs also work with the arrow keys.
- **Visit:** opening hours with today highlighted, the address and transit notes, and a small illustrated street map.
- **Reserve:** a booking form whose time slots follow each day's hours and that skips Mondays (closed). Submitting shows a confirmation message, but nothing is actually sent; it's front-end only.

The layout adjusts for tablet and phone screens.

To use it for a real restaurant, change the name, address, phone and the hours. The hours appear in two places: the `HOURS` object in the script and the hours list in the HTML.
