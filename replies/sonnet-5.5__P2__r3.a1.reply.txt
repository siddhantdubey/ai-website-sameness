jI've written the landing page to `index.html` in the working directory. It's a single file with inline CSS, a small inline script, and inline SVG, so there are no external fonts, images, or libraries.

**What's on the page**
- **Hero:** a tomato-and-gold gradient with a headline, two calls to action, and a gingham-stripe divider.
- **Our Story:** a short narrative, three quick facts, and an SVG plate of pasta.
- **Menu:** antipasti, pasta, secondi, and dolci, with dotted price leaders and vegetarian tags.
- **Pull quote:** a regular's line on an olive band.
- **Visit:** hours, address, phone, and a reservation form.
- **Accessibility and layout:** it's responsive and keeps keyboard focus visible. It also respects reduced-motion settings.

**Things to know**
- **Placeholder content:** "Osteria Fiorella", the address, phone number, hours, menu items and prices, the quote, and "since 2009" are all invented. Swap in the real details.
- **Reservation form:** it has no backend. Submitting shows a confirmation message in the page, but nothing is sent anywhere. To make it work, point it at a booking service (OpenTable, Resy, Tock), a form endpoint, or your own server.
- **Photos:** I used CSS and SVG instead of photography. Real food photos in the hero and story sections would improve it considerably. You could embed them as base64 to keep the file self-contained.
- **Not tested in a browser:** I haven't opened it, so give it a quick look on desktop and mobile.
