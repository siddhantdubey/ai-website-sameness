I've built the landing page as a single file, `index.html`, with no external dependencies. I haven't opened it in a browser to check it.

**What's on the page:**
- **Top of the page:** a sticky header with navigation (it collapses to a menu on phones) and an opening section with a "Free Consultation" button and an office card showing phone, address and hours.
- **Credibility:** a row of firm stats, four practice areas with short service tags, and a three-step "How We Work" section with a note on fees.
- **People and proof:** profiles for three attorneys and client testimonials with the usual disclaimer that results vary.
- **Questions and contact:** an FAQ that expands and collapses, and a consultation request form that checks required fields before it can be submitted.
- **Footer:** contact details and an "Attorney Advertising" notice.

**Look:** warm off-white background, serif headings, and deep red and dark green accents. It works on phones, can be used by keyboard, and turns off animations for visitors who have reduced motion enabled.

**Placeholders to replace:**
- The firm name "Marlow & Reyes", the attorneys, testimonials, phone number, address and email are all made up. The email uses a `.example` domain.
- The attorney photos are boxes showing initials; swap in real photos.
