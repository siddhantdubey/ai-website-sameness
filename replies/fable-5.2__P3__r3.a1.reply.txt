The portfolio is in `index.html` in the working directory: one file, no external requests, no build step. I checked that the markup is well-formed, but I have not opened it in a browser, so the layout and the viewer are untested visually.

The photographer is a placeholder I invented: Odile Brandt, an architecture and landscape photographer in Porto.

- **Work**: a grid of nine photographs plus a wide hero image, with filters for Buildings, Coast and Interiors.
- **Viewer**: clicking any photograph opens it full screen with previous/next buttons and arrow-key navigation.
- **About**: a short bio, a facts list (base, commissions, cameras, clients, prints) and a three-step outline of how a commission runs.
- **Contact**: a large mailto link and a copy-address button.
- **Lights toggle**: switches between a light and a dark background, and follows the system setting until the visitor chooses.

Before using it, replace these:

- **The photographs are drawings.** With no real images to embed, each one is an inline SVG scene with film grain over it. To use real photos, replace the `<svg>` inside each `<span class="frame">` with an `<img>` and add `.frame > img { width:100%; height:100%; object-fit:cover }`. The viewer script clones the SVG, so it also needs a small change to clone the `<img>` instead.
- **All the copy is made up.** That includes the name, bio, client list, captions, the January 2027 availability line and the email address (`studio@odilebrandt.example`).
