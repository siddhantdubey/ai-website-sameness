Here's the page: `index.html`. It's one self-contained file with no external fonts, images, or scripts.

**The restaurant:** *Osteria Lucciola* ("firefly"), a 22-table family spot on a made-up Calloway Street.

**What's on the page**
- **Striped awning header**, built in CSS, with a scalloped edge.
- **Hero** with a headline, two call-to-action buttons, and an **open/closed indicator** that uses the visitor's local time against the posted hours.
- **Tonight's specials** on a hanging chalkboard, stamped with today's date.
- **Our Story** section with an inline SVG illustration (a windowsill at dusk) and a few quick facts.
- **Menu** with Antipasti, Pasta, Secondi and Dolci tabs. Prices use dotted leaders, and dishes are tagged "Vegetarian" or "House." The tabs work with arrow keys.
- **Sunday Supper** feature: a family-style dinner at a fixed price. Its button pre-fills the form with the next Sunday and that occasion.
- **Reviews**, **hours table** (today's row is highlighted), and a small SVG map.
- **Reservation form**:
  - Time slots follow each day's hours.
  - Mondays are blocked, and times that have already passed today are hidden.
  - Submitting shows a friendly confirmation.
- **Footer** with a newsletter signup.

The layout adjusts for phones, and animations are turned off for anyone who has "reduce motion" set on their device.

**Placeholders to swap before going live:** the name, address, phone, email, menu and prices, reviews, and social links are all made up. The reservation and newsletter forms only work on the page itself and don't send anything anywhere. To take real bookings, point the form at a booking service like Resy or OpenTable, or at your own backend.
