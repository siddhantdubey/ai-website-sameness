The landing page is in `index.html` in the working directory: one file, no external fonts, images or scripts. I couldn't open it in a browser here (none installed), so the layout is unchecked visually; the only check was that the script parses.

The product is a fictional app called **Plumb** ("Projects that hang straight"), built around a schedule that reflows when a task slips. The page has:

- **Hero:** a working product demo with Timeline, Board and List tabs. Ticking a task off in List updates the other two views and the progress counter.
- **Features:** four features, each with a small inline illustration.
- **How it works:** a three-step walkthrough of what happens when a task runs late, plus a customer quote.
- **Pricing:** three tiers with a yearly/monthly toggle.
- **FAQ:** five expandable questions.
- **Sign-up:** an email form as the closing call to action, then a footer.

It collapses to a single column on small screens and respects reduced-motion settings.

Before this goes anywhere real, replace the placeholders:

- **Invented content:** the name, copy, prices, the testimonial and its attribution, and the FAQ answers (import sources, data regions, trial terms) are all made up.
- **Sign-up form:** it only shows a confirmation message and sends the email nowhere.
- **Dead links:** Sign in just jumps to the sign-up form, and several footer links (Changelog, Docs, Privacy, Terms) point to `#`.
