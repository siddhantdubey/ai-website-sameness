I built the landing page as `index.html` in the working directory: one file, no external requests, system fonts only. I haven't opened it in a browser, so the layout is unchecked visually.

The page is for a made-up product called Tack, whose pitch is that it keeps the original plan next to the current one so you can see when a project started slipping.

- **Hero:** a yellow band with a working timeline mock. It opens on the original plan, then slides to today's dates with the slip marked on each task; the two buttons switch between them.
- **Features:** three sections, each with its own small piece of UI (a due-date history, a "waiting on" chain, the Monday email) rather than icon cards.
- **Rest of the page:** a "Small on purpose" list of things the product doesn't do, one quote, two-plan pricing, a short FAQ and a closing call to action.

Everything about the product is placeholder: the name, the customer count, the testimonial, the prices and the footer details are all invented. All buttons and links point to `#`.
