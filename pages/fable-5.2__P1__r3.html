Questions

Before you switch

Can we bring our existing projects?

Yes. Import from Trello, Jira or any CSV. Columns, assignees, due dates and comments come across, and you can preview the result before anything is saved.

What counts as a guest?

Clients, contractors and anyone else you invite to a single project. Guests can comment and update tasks assigned to them, and they never count toward your bill.

What happens when the Team trial ends?

You drop back to Starter automatically. Nothing is deleted, and Team features unlock again whenever you upgrade.

Can we leave and take our data?

Any time. Export every project to CSV or JSON from workspace settings, with attachments included.
