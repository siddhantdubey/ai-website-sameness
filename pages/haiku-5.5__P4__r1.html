About the Firm

A firm built on trust and personal attention

Whitfield & Clarke was founded on a simple belief: every client deserves a lawyer who listens, explains clearly, and fights as hard as they would for themselves.

As a small firm, we keep our caseloads manageable so you always know who is handling your matter and can reach them directly. No call-center runarounds, no surprise billing.

  • Direct access to your attorney
  • Transparent fees with no hidden costs
  • Plain-language updates throughout your case
