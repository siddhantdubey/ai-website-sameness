How we work

Plain steps, plain language, no surprises on the bill.

Legal work feels easier when you know what comes next. Here is what to expect from the first call onward.

On fees: we quote flat fees for defined projects and give written hourly estimates for everything else. You'll never be billed for the intro call.

  1. A short, free conversation

    Tell us what's going on. We'll tell you whether we're the right fit and what the realistic options look like.

  2. A written scope and price

    If we proceed, you receive a clear engagement letter covering what we'll do, who does it, and what it costs.

  3. Steady updates

    You'll hear from us at each milestone, and your questions get answered by an attorney, not a ticket queue.

  4. A clean finish

    We close out with a summary of what was done, your documents in order, and guidance on what to do next.

Common questions

Before you call

Is the first consultation really free?

Yes. The initial 20-minute call is free and carries no obligation. It helps us understand your situation and decide together whether to work together.

What should I prepare for our first meeting?

A short summary of what happened and what outcome you want, plus any relevant documents such as contracts, notices, or correspondence.

Do you offer flat fees?

For many defined projects, such as entity formation, wills, and lease reviews, we do. Otherwise we provide a written estimate before work begins.

Do you meet remotely?

Yes. We meet by phone, video, or in person at our office, whichever suits you.

Is what I tell you confidential?

Communications with a prospective client are treated as confidential. Please avoid including sensitive details in the web form; save them for a call.
