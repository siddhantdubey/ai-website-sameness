§ 01 — Features

Everything a project needs. Nothing it has to fight through.

Most tools give you more fields to fill in. Ledgerline gives you fewer, and makes the ones that matter impossible to ignore.

01

Boards that stay honest

Cards that sit untouched for a week quietly flag themselves. No more columns full of work nobody is doing.

02

Timelines with dependencies

Drag a date and everything downstream moves with it. See the real launch date before your stakeholders ask.

03

Status updates, drafted

Every Friday, Ledgerline drafts a plain-English summary of what shipped, what slipped, and why. You edit; it sends.

04

Workload you can see

A capacity view shows who's overloaded next week — so you rebalance on Monday instead of apologizing on Thursday.

05

Goals tied to tasks

Link quarterly goals to the actual work. Progress rolls up automatically, so OKR reviews take minutes, not afternoons.

06

Plays well with others

Two-way sync with GitHub, Slack, Figma, Google Calendar, and 60+ more. Updates flow in; you stop copy-pasting.

§ 04 — Questions

Asked and answered.

Can we import our existing projects?

Yes. One-click importers bring over boards, tasks, assignees, comments, and due dates from Trello, Asana, Jira, Monday, and any CSV. Most teams are fully moved in under an hour.

How do drafted status updates work?

Every week, Ledgerline reads task changes, completed work, and slipped dates on your project, then writes a short summary in plain English. Nothing is sent until someone on your team reviews and approves it.

Is our data secure?

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, we're SOC 2 Type II audited, and Organization plans include SSO, SCIM provisioning, and full audit logs. You can export or delete everything at any time.

What happens when the free trial ends?

Your workspace drops back to the Starter plan. Nothing is deleted — you just lose access to Team features until you upgrade.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits and education?

Yes — 50% off any paid plan for registered nonprofits and accredited schools. Reach out to our team with your details.
