Biography & Practice

I approach every commission with patience and natural light first—allowing architecture, landscape, and human presence to settle before releasing the shutter.

Trained in architectural photography in Zurich before establishing my studio in Copenhagen, I work collaboratively with editorial directors, architects, ceramicists, and hospitality founders across Europe, North America, and Japan. Every project can be delivered digitally alongside hand-printed darkroom contact sheets and archival pigment editions.
