Before we meet

A little clarity,
from the start.

What happens in the first conversation?

We discuss the general nature of your matter and whether our firm may be a good fit. We’ll explain any consultation fee before scheduling and outline the next steps.

What should I prepare?

A brief overview, any important dates, and the questions you want to ask are a helpful start. We’ll let you know which documents to bring and how to share them securely.

How do your fees work?

Fees depend on the type and scope of your matter. Before an engagement begins, we provide a written agreement explaining the work, billing arrangements, and any anticipated expenses.

When do you become my attorney?

Contacting us does not create an attorney-client relationship. Representation begins only after we complete our intake process and both parties agree to a written engagement.
