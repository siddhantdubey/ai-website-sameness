Behind the camera

Drawn to the ordinary.
Looking for the extraordinary.

I’m Alex, an independent photographer with a soft spot for open landscapes, thoughtful spaces, and the kind of light you can’t plan for.

My work is about paying attention. Whether I’m photographing a distant coastline or a quiet corner of a city, I’m looking for images that feel honest, unhurried, and a little unexpected.
