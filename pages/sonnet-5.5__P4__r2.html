Common questions

Before you call.

Not seeing your question? Send us a note and we'll respond within one business day.

Is the first consultation really free?

Yes. Our initial 20-minute call is free and carries no obligation. We'll tell you honestly whether we can help, and if not, try to point you to someone who can.

How do your fees work?

Where possible we offer flat fees for defined work such as wills, entity formation, and closings. For ongoing or contested matters we bill hourly, always with a written estimate and engagement letter up front.

Will I work directly with an attorney?

Always. Every matter is handled by a named attorney who is your point of contact from start to finish.

Do you meet clients remotely?

Yes. We offer secure video meetings and electronic signing, as well as in-person appointments at our office.

Is what I tell you confidential?

Yes. Information you share when seeking legal advice is protected by attorney–client confidentiality, subject to applicable law.
