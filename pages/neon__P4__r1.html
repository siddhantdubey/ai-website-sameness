How We Work

Deliberate process. Transparent economics.

Working with legal counsel should reduce uncertainty—not add to it. Every new engagement follows three clear steps.

01

Confidential Intake & Conflict Check

Speak directly with a partner for an initial 30-minute assessment. We clear conflicts promptly, listen to your objectives, and tell you candidly whether we are the right firm for the matter.

02

Written Scope & Strategy Memo

Before substantive work begins, you receive a plain-English engagement letter outlining milestones, realistic timelines, decision points, and our proposed fee structure.

03

Partner-Led Execution

Your lead attorney manages communication, drafting, negotiation, and advocacy directly—keeping you informed on a predictable cadence with zero hand-offs to unfamiliar junior staff.

Practical Fee Arrangements

Depending on the matter, we offer flat fees for estate plans and standard corporate formations, phased budgets for commercial litigation, and predictable monthly retainers for outside general counsel clients.
