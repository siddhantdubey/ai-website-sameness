03 // BIOGRAPHY & METHODOLOGY

The Practice

FIG. A — SØREN LINDQVIST VESTERBRO DARKROOM, CPH

“I approach buildings as slow portraits and portraits as lived architecture. Wait long enough in any space, and the light will tell you what the structure is trying to hold together.”

Trained initially in architectural conservation at the Royal Danish Academy before turning full-time to photography in 2016, Søren Lindqvist brings a structural discipline to editorial and commercial image-making. Working strictly with natural and continuous ambient light, his frames prioritize tonal depth, tactile grain, and unhurried stillness.

Alongside commissioned assignments for architectural practices, fashion houses, and print journals, Søren maintains a personal darkroom in Copenhagen where he hand-prints silver gelatin editions and oversees drum scanning for large-scale museum exhibitions.
