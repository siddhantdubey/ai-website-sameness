Practice & Background

A slower way of looking.

“My work begins with waiting—for the last ten minutes of blue hour, for shadow to bisect concrete, or for a room to settle into silence.”

Trained in architectural studies in Copenhagen before turning fully to photography, Maren Lindholm brings a spatial sensibility to every assignment. Whether commissioned for a monograph on Nordic timber architecture or an atmospheric editorial essay, her photographs prioritize natural light, disciplined composition, and tactile tonal depth.

All fine-art editions are hand-inspected and printed on archival Hahnemühle Photo Rag 308gsm in small numbered runs, signed and accompanied by a certificate of authenticity.

Primary Kit: Hasselblad 500C/M (80mm f/2.8 Planar), Leica M11 (35mm & 50mm Summilux), Kodak Portra 400, Cinestill 800T, and Ilford HP5 Plus.
