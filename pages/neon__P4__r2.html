Working With Us

Common questions before an initial call.

Clear expectations make for a stronger attorney-client relationship. Here is how intake, conflicts, and billing work at Whitaker & Sato LLP.

After we run a quick conflicts check, you speak directly with a partner for 30 minutes (by video, phone, or in our Boston office). We review your goals, identify immediate legal risks or deadlines, and outline proposed scope and fee options so you can decide whether we are the right fit.

For estate planning, entity formations, contract reviews, and many commercial leases, we offer transparent flat fees. For litigation and M&A transactions, we provide clear phase-based budgets and hourly or capped-fee engagements—always documented in a written engagement letter before work starts.
