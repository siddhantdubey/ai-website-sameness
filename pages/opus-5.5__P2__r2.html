Our story

Not fancy. Just family, every night since ’87.

When Pia Morello arrived from Modena with a rolling pin and a shoebox of handwritten recipes, she opened a twelve-seat room on this corner and fed the whole block. The tables multiplied; the recipes didn’t change.

Today her grandson Luca runs the kitchen, still rolling sfoglia by hand every morning on her original walnut board. His sister Giulia runs the floor and the wine list — mostly small growers, mostly unfiltered, always fairly priced.

We know most of our guests by name, and the ones we don’t, we’d like to.
