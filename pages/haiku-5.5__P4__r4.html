About the Firm

A smaller firm by design.

Large firms often hand clients off to junior associates and bill by the hour without end. We chose a different path. At Hartwell & Lane, you work directly with the attorney handling your matter, and you will always know what is happening and what it will cost.

Since 2009, we have built our practice on referrals from satisfied clients and on the courtroom and boardroom results that come from careful preparation.

  • Direct accessYour attorney answers your calls and emails, not a rotating team.
  • Plain languageWe explain options and trade-offs so you can make informed decisions.
  • Clear feesFlat fees where possible, and written estimates before we begin.
  • IntegrityWe will tell you when you do not need a lawyer, or when a different one is a better fit.
