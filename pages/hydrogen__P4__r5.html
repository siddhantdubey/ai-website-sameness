Representative Matters

Selected engagements & outcomes.

A cross-section of recent matters illustrating how we protect client capital, property rights, and family continuity.

Commercial Litigation

Closely Held Software Firm Buyout

Represented a 40% co-founder facing an attempted freeze-out and disputed valuation prior to an acquisition offer.

Outcome: Negotiated $3.1M structured buyout prior to summary judgment.
Real Estate & Land Court

Mixed-Use Easement & Boundary Action

Defended a commercial landlord in Suffolk Land Court against a prescriptive easement claim threatening loading-dock access.

Outcome: Judgment for landlord confirming clear title and recorded license terms.
Trusts & Succession

Third-Generation Manufacturer Transition

Structured voting and non-voting recapitalization, grantor trusts, and buy-sell agreements for an operating business.

Outcome: Seamless leadership transfer with unified family governance charter.
Contract & Ch. 93A

Commercial Construction Delay Defense

Represented a general contractor in a multi-party mechanics lien and delay-damages suit involving a Back Bay renovation.

Outcome: Dismissed Ch. 93A treble-damages count; settled at 18% of initial demand.
Commercial Leasing

Medical Office Portfolio Restructuring

Negotiated build-out allowances, SNDA protections, and early-termination caps across four Greater Boston clinical leases.

Outcome: Closed $8.4M aggregate lease commitments on schedule.
Probate Litigation

Contested Fiduciary Accounting Resolution

Represented successor co-trustees resolving multi-year trust accounting objections among five beneficiaries.

Outcome: Court-approved settlement avoiding protracted probate trial.
