La Nostra Storia

Flour, eggs, patience, and a lively dining room.

When Giulia and Matteo Marchetti opened twenty-eight years ago with twelve tables and a second-hand pasta extruder, their goal was simple: cook the Sunday food of Emilia-Romagna and Piedmont with Hudson Valley produce, and pour honest wines without ceremony.

Today their daughter Clara leads the kitchen alongside Chef Luca Bellini. We still mill heritage semolina in-house, roast whole branzino over applewood embers, and save a handful of marble counter seats every night for neighbors dropping in unannounced.
