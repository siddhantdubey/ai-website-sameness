Our Story

Three generations, one table.

Trattoria Rosa opened in 1987 as a six-table room on the corner of Maple and Fourth. Nonna Rosa rolled the first batch of tagliatelle by hand, and her recipe for Sunday ragù is still the one we simmer for eight hours every week.

Today the kitchen is run by her grandson, Marco, who still makes the pasta every morning before service. We source flour from a mill two hours north, tomatoes from a family farm in the valley, and olive oil from a grove our cousins tend in Puglia.

We are a neighborhood restaurant. Walk-ins are welcome, kids get crayons, and the staff knows what you ordered last time.

— Marco & the Rosa family
