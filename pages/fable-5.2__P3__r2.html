About

I photograph places at the hours when nobody is asking anything of them: dunes before the wind, housing blocks at lunch, kitchens after the guests have gone.

I grew up between Budapest and Porto and have worked as a photographer for eleven years. Most of what I make is commissioned — magazine stories, buildings for the architects who drew them, portraits of people who would rather not be photographed.

I shoot medium-format film where time allows and digital where it doesn't. I scan and print everything myself, so the person you brief is the person who delivers the files.
