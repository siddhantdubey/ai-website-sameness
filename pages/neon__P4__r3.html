01 — The Boutique Difference

Big-firm caliber without the big-firm machinery.

Large law firms are built to leverage volume—assigning complex commercial matters to rotating teams of first-year associates. We built Harlow & Hale LLP around the opposite premise: leaner teams, sharper judgment, and direct accountability.

“Good counsel tells you what the law says. Exceptional counsel helps you see three moves ahead and protect the business you spent decades building.”
— Eleanor Harlow, Managing Partner
I.

No Hand-Offs After the Pitch

The partner you speak with on day one drafts the dispositive brief, negotiates the operating agreement, and stands beside you in court. You never subsidize a training curve.

II.

Trial Readiness Creates Leverage

Most business disputes settle, but settlement terms depend on credibility. Opposing counsel knows which firms file boilerplate motions and which prepare every record for verdict.

III.

Disciplined, Predictable Fee Structures

We align legal spend with business reality through phased litigation budgets, capped transactional fees, and outside general counsel retainers—with candid early case assessments.
