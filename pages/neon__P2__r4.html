La Famiglia · Since 1994

Thirty years of flour-dusted mornings on Henry Street.

When Nonna Lucia and Grandfather Paolo opened Osteria Santoro with nine checkered tables and a single battered brass pasta cutter, their promise was simple: cook the food of Emilia-Romagna and Campania with ingredients sourced as faithfully as if we were still back in Parma and Naples.

Today, siblings Matteo and Elena run the dining room and kitchen with the exact same rhythm. Stock simmers overnight, tomatoes are crushed by hand, and half the room still knows each other by first name before the antipasti arrive.

Sfoglia Fresca

Organic stone-ground semolina and bright-yolk Hudson Valley eggs rolled out on our maple pasta table every morning at 9 AM.

La Dispensa

DOP San Marzano tomatoes, 36-month Parmigiano-Reggiano, and cold-pressed Sicilian olive oil paired with peak greenmarket produce.

La Tavola

Never rushed, never stuffy. Pull up a stool for a plate of cacio e pepe and a glass of Barbera, or linger over three courses with family.
