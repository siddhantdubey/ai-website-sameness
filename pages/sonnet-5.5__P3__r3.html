02 — About

I wait for the
in-between moments.

I'm Mara, a self-taught photographer who traded a desk job for a camera bag in 2014. My work leans on natural light, patience and conversation. The best frames usually happen after the "official" shot is done.

Clients include independent magazines, hospitality brands, musicians and families. Whether it's a two-hour portrait session or a three-week editorial assignment, I bring the same calm, prepared approach.
