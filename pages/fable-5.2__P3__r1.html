About

I work slowly, with one camera and usually one lens, and I would rather come back on a better day than fix it afterwards.

I grew up in Oban and trained as an architectural technician before I picked up a camera for site surveys and never quite put it down. Since going freelance in 2019 I have split my time between magazine commissions, long building surveys for architects, and personal projects in the west Highlands.

Most of my work is made on 6×6 film and scanned at my studio in Dennistoun. I shoot digital when a deadline or a client needs it, and I will tell you honestly which suits your job.
