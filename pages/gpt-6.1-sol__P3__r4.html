A little about me

I’m Alex. A photographer drawn to ordinary things, unfamiliar roads, and the way light changes everything.

Based in the Pacific Northwest, I make images with a sense of place and a human connection. My practice moves between personal explorations and thoughtful collaborations with people and brands.
