03 / Ethos & Practice

“Photography is an exercise in subtraction—removing noise until only geometry, atmosphere, and honest emotion remain in the frame.”

Over the past eleven years, Soren Lindqvist has split his time between commercial assignments in Western Europe and slow, self-assigned architectural documentation across the Nordic region.

Trained originally in industrial design before turning to the darkroom, his approach favors deliberate pacing, natural window illumination, and tactile hand-printed silver gelatin proofs.
