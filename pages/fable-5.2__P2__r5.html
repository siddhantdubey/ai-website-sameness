La famiglia

Thirty years, one corner

“If the pot's on, there's enough for one more.”
Rosalba Moretti, who still checks the sauce

Rosalba Moretti opened Lucciola in 1994 with eleven tables, her mother's rolling pin from Abruzzo, and a handwritten menu taped to the door. She named it after the fireflies in the field behind the house she grew up in, the ones that came out just as dinner hit the table.

Her children Carla and Enzo run the room and the kitchen now. The rolling pin is still in use. So is the rule that nobody from the neighborhood waits outside in the rain: if the tables are full, you get a glass of wine and a spot by the pass until one opens.

We buy tomatoes from the Saturday market two blocks over, bread from Halloran's across the street, and we've never once changed the tiramisù.
