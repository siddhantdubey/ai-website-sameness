Common Questions

Before you call.

Don't see your question? Ask it during your free consultation — there's no obligation to hire us.

Is the first consultation really free?

Yes. Your first 20‑minute conversation with an attorney is free and confidential. We'll discuss your situation, explain your options, and tell you whether we're the right fit.

How do your fees work?

Many services — wills, trusts, business formation, and residential closings — are offered at a flat fee quoted in writing. Litigation and open‑ended matters are billed hourly, with a written estimate and monthly itemized statements.

Do you offer virtual or evening appointments?

Yes. We meet by video or phone, and we hold evening appointments on Tuesdays and Thursdays until 7:30 p.m. for clients who can't get away during the workday.

What should I bring to my first meeting?

Any documents related to your matter — existing wills, contracts, leases, court papers, or correspondence. We'll send a short checklist when you book.

Do you handle criminal or personal injury cases?

We don't. If your matter falls outside our practice areas, we're happy to refer you to a trusted colleague who specializes in it.
