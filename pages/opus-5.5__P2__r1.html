Our Story

Cooking like someone’s grandmother is watching.

Fiorella Bianchi opened this room in 1998 with a borrowed pasta machine, her mother’s recipe box from Bologna, and a promise to the neighbors that dinner would never be fussy. Today her son Marco runs the kitchen, and the recipe box is still on the shelf above the stove.

We buy tomatoes from the Saturday market, flour from a mill two counties over, and cheese from people we know by name. The menu changes when the season does. The prices change as little as we can manage.
