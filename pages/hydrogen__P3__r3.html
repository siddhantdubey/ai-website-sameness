03 / Practice & Darkroom

“I wait for the exact ten minutes when architecture stops looking permanent and begins behaving like weather.”

Trained in architectural history in Lyon before spending five years assisting commercial directors in Paris, Clara works with natural daylight, minimal crew footprints, and hand-printed archival baryta proofs.

Available globally for architectural monographs, design hospitality, cultural institutions, and intimate editorial portraits. All fine-art editions are signed, numbered, and accompanied by a certificate of provenance.
