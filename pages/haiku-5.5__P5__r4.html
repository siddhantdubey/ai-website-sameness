Questions

Things people ask before they switch.

Can we import from our current tool?

Yes. Hitchpost reads CSV exports and the public APIs of the common trackers. Imports keep assignees, due dates, and parent-child links. Attachments are copied over on the Team plan and above.

Where is our data stored?

On the cloud plans, data is stored in the region you pick when you create the workspace. On the self-hosted option, it stays on your own infrastructure and we never see it.

Does it work offline?

You can read and edit cards offline in the desktop and mobile apps. Changes sync the next time you connect. Conflicts are shown side by side so nothing is silently overwritten.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes. Monthly plans can be cancelled from the billing page and take effect at the end of the period. Annual plans are refunded pro rata within the first 30 days.
