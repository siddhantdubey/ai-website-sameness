Our story

A kitchen built on one grandmother’s recipes.

Fiorella opened with eight tables, a borrowed pasta machine, and Nonna Fiorella’s handwritten recipe cards. Over a decade later the cards are laminated, the tables number twenty-two, and the sauce is still started before sunrise.

We buy flour from a mill two hours north, vegetables from the Saturday market down the street, and everything else from people whose names we know. Nothing fancy, just done properly.

— The Marchetti family
