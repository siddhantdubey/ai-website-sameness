About the Firm

A small firm by design. Personal by principle.

Northgate Law Group was founded on a simple belief: legal representation should feel human. Large firms often hand clients off to junior staff. We do things differently. When you call, you speak with the attorney who will be handling your matter.

We are a small team, which means fewer layers between you and your lawyer, faster responses, and a level of attention that is hard to find elsewhere.
