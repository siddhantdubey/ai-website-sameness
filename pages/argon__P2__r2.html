La Nostra Storia

Built for Tuesday nights as much as Saturday celebrations.

When brother and sister Marco and Sofia Bellini opened Trattoria del Vicino in a former corner bakery in 2011, the goal was simple: create the kind of unpretentious neighborhood room we grew up with between Bologna and Rome.

We keep a quarter of our dining room and our entire 14-seat Carrara marble bar unreserved every evening so neighbors can always wander in for a plate of cacio e pepe and a carafe of chilled Lambrusco without planning weeks ahead.
