The weekly loop

Most tools track tasks. Cadence tracks momentum.

Every project in Cadence runs on a one-week loop. It's short enough to course-correct and long enough to ship something real.

01 — MONDAY

Plan

Pull work from the backlog into the week. Cadence flags overcommitment before it happens, based on what your team actually finished last time.

  • Capacity forecast per person
  • One-click carry-over from last week
  • Async planning — no meeting required
02 — TUE → THU

Ship

Move cards, link PRs, leave notes. Blockers bubble up automatically to the people who can clear them, not to a channel everyone mutes.

  • GitHub, GitLab & Figma sync
  • Smart blocker routing
  • Focus mode hides everything else
03 — FRIDAY

Review

Cadence drafts the weekly review from what actually moved: what shipped, what slipped, and why. Edit it, then send it to stakeholders in one click.

  • Auto-drafted weekly summaries
  • Slip analysis with root causes
  • Shareable read-only links

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Something else on your mind? Our team replies within one business day.

Do we have to work in weekly cycles?+

The week is the default because it works for most teams, but you can set any project to run on 2-week cycles, or switch the loop off and use Cadence as a straightforward board and timeline.

How does the auto-drafted review work?+

On Friday, Cadence looks at what moved: cards shipped, PRs merged, dates that slipped, and the comments explaining why. It writes a short summary you can edit before sharing. Nothing gets sent without your approval.

Can we import from our current tool?+

Yes. One-click importers for Jira, Trello, Asana and Linear bring over tasks, assignees, comments and attachments. Most teams are fully moved in under an hour.

Is our data secure?+

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Organization plans add SSO, SCIM, audit logs and a choice of EU or US data residency.

What happens when the free plan isn't enough?+

When you add your 9th member we'll ask you to choose a plan. Your data never gets locked or deleted, and you can downgrade at any time.
