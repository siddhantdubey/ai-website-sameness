Our Story

Three Generations at the Stove

What started as a six-table dining room with a single handmade pasta board is now the place where the neighborhood gathers for birthdays, first dates, and Tuesday dinners that turn into Sunday.

We still make our pasta by hand every morning, still knead our dough by feel, and still send out a plate of something the chef is excited about, because that's how Nonna did it.

— Marco & the Rossi family
