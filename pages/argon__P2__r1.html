La Nostra Filosofia

No white-tablecloth stiffness. Just slow simmers & honest hospitality.

When Marco and Elena Bellini opened Trattoria del Vicolo in a former corner bakery in 2014, their rule was simple: never serve a pasta shape we didn’t roll that morning, and never rush a table that’s still finishing their carafe of Montepulciano.

Our kitchen bridges Elena’s upbringing in Bologna—rich egg doughs, slow-braised meats, and 36-month Parmigiano Vacche Rosse—with Marco’s Roman roots: crackly wood-fired crust, bitter winter greens, sharp Pecorino Romano, and abundant black pepper.

Il Mattarello

Pastas extruded in bronze dies or rolled by hand on porous maple tables so sauce clings to every fold.
