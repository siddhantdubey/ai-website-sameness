A little bit of Nonna. A lot of Brooklyn.

Casa Lilla started with a pasta roller, a borrowed recipe book, and a big table on a small Brooklyn block. Today, we still make the pasta by hand, pour the wine generously, and believe the best meals happen when nobody’s in a hurry.

Come celebrate, catch up, or make an ordinary Tuesday feel like something special. There’s always room for one more.
