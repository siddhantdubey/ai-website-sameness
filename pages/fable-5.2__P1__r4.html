How it works

From first plank to open water in an afternoon.

A slipway is the ramp a ship is built on and launched from. Yours takes about twenty minutes to set up.

  1. Lay the keel

    Import your backlog or start from a template. Set a target date and tell Slipway who's on the crew.

  2. Build the hull

    Work in the view you like — timeline, board or list. They're the same data, so nobody has to keep two tools in sync.

  3. Launch

    Watch the forecast close in on the target. When they meet, break the bottle. Then archive the project and start the next.

Questions

Asked before boarding.

How does the forecast actually work?

Slipway adds up the estimated work left, divides it by the capacity your team really has (accounting for time off and other projects), and adjusts for how your past estimates compared with reality. No estimates yet? It starts from task counts and sharpens as you go.

Can we bring our existing projects?

Yes. There are one-click importers for Jira, Trello and Asana, plus CSV for everything else. Assignees, due dates, statuses and comments come across.

Do clients and stakeholders need a paid seat?

No. Guests can view projects, comment and receive the Friday digest for free on Schooner and Fleet. You only pay for people who own or edit tasks.

What happens to our data if we leave?

You can export everything to CSV or JSON at any time, on any plan. After cancellation we keep your workspace for 30 days in case you change your mind, then delete it.

Is there a discount for nonprofits and schools?

Schooner is half price for registered nonprofits and free for classrooms. Write to us from your organisation's address and we'll set it up.
