§ 2

How we work, and what it costs.

Most of what we do has a flat fee, quoted in writing before we start. Where a matter has to be billed hourly, you get an estimate first and a statement every month.

  1. A free half-hour conversation

    In person, by phone or by video. You explain the situation; we tell you whether you need a lawyer at all, and if so, what we would do.

  2. A written scope and fee

    One page setting out the work, the price, and the expected timeline. Nothing begins until you have signed it.

  3. The work, with one point of contact

    The attorney you met is the attorney who does the work. You have a direct line and email, and we return messages within one business day.

§ 4

Questions people ask before they call.

If yours is not here, ask it at the consultation.

Is the first consultation really free?

Yes. Thirty minutes, with an attorney, at no charge and with no obligation to hire us. If we run long because the conversation is useful, we do not start a clock.

What should I bring?

Any documents you already have: a contract, a letter you received, an existing will, a court notice. If you have nothing on paper yet, come anyway. A short written timeline of what has happened is always helpful.

Will what I tell you be kept confidential?

Yes. What you tell us in a consultation is confidential even if you decide not to hire us. Please do not send sensitive details through the website form, though; save them for the conversation.

Do you take cases outside your four practice areas?

No. We do not handle criminal defense, personal injury, immigration or bankruptcy. We keep a list of lawyers we know and trust in each of those fields and are glad to refer you.

Can I pay in installments?

For flat-fee work over $1,000, yes: half at signing and the balance over up to three months, without interest.
