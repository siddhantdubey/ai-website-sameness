Our Craft

Flour, Water & Patience

01

Morning Pasta Bench

Every morning our sfogline knead organic stone-ground semolina and pasture-raised egg yolks into bronze-extruded rigatoni, hand-pinched agnolotti, and paper-thin tajarin.

02

Family Farms & Italian Groves

We pair Hudson Valley greens, wild mushrooms, and day-boat seafood with San Marzano DOP tomatoes, aged Parmigiano-Reggiano, and cold-pressed Tuscan olive oil.

03

Hearth & Hospitality

Our oak-fired hearth chars sourdough focaccia and crisps whole market fish while our front room keeps ten counter stools open every night for spontaneous neighbors.
