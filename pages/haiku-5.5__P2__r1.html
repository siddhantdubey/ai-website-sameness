Our Story

Three generations, one wooden table.

Trattoria Oliva began in 1998 as a six-table kitchen on Maple Street, run by Lucia Oliva and her sons. The recipes came from her mother's village kitchen outside Bologna: tagliatelle rolled by hand each morning, ragù that simmers from breakfast until dinner, and bread that never leaves the oven under-proofed.

Today we are still a family table at heart. Our dining room seats fifty, our wine list leans toward small Italian producers, and our regulars still call ahead to ask whether the cacio e pepe is on. (It usually is.)

— The Oliva family
