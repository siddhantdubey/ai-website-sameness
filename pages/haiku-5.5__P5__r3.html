How it runs

Four rules that keep the schedule honest.

  1. 01

    One owner per card

    Every task has exactly one name on it. Shared responsibility is where work goes to stall, so Furrow won't let you assign two people to the same card.

  2. 02

    Dates follow dependencies

    Push a blocker and every downstream date shifts with it. The change appears as a readable diff, and nothing moves until you accept it.

  3. 03

    A timeline in weeks, not Gantt bars

    Drag to reschedule. Each Monday you get one short list of the things that slipped, why they slipped, and who owns the fix.

  4. 04

    Notifications that wait

    A digest arrives at 9 a.m. in your timezone. Direct mentions interrupt you, and everything else waits for the digest.

Limits

What Furrow is not for.

What it does well

  • Keeps tasks, owners, and dates in sync
  • Recalculates schedules when dependencies move
  • Exports to CSV and iCal, and imports from Linear, Trello, and Jira
  • Works offline in the desktop app and syncs on reconnect

What it doesn't do

  • Track billable time or generate invoices
  • Roll up reporting across dozens of portfolios
  • Replace your documentation tool
  • Edit boards on a phone (read-only on mobile)
