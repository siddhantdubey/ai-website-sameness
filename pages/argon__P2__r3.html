La Nostra Filosofia

A dining room that feels like an extension of your own kitchen.

When siblings Marco and Elena Bellini opened Osteria del Vicolo eleven years ago, the promise was simple: never serve a dish that requires pretension to enjoy, and never rush a table that is still talking.

Every morning at 7:00 AM, our sfoglina rolls out sheets of golden egg-yolk dough on maple boards. Our olive oil is pressed by a single family cooperative outside Lucca, and our bread is naturally leavened for 36 hours before hitting the hearth stones.

Riti di Quartiere

Weekly Neighborhood Rituals

A real neighborhood trattoria belongs to its regulars. Join us for the traditions that mark the rhythm of our week.

Tue – Fri • 4:30 – 6:00 PM 01

L’Ora dell’Aperitivo

Grab a stool at the marble bar after work. Every Campari Spritz, Americano, or glass of Franciacorta comes with a complimentary wooden board of warm focaccia, Castelvetrano olives, and mortadella.

No reservations needed at the bar

Every Sunday • From 5:00 PM 02

Sunday Nonna Supper

Our wood oven bakes a limited 24 portions of 12-layer Lasagna Verde alla Bolognese alongside a family-style bowl of bitter greens and a carafe of barbera for $45 per guest.

Sells out early — note lasagna when booking

Nightly • After 9:00 PM 03

Late-Night Cacio e Pepe

For night owls, industry neighbors, and post-theater diners: a steaming bowl of hand-rolled tonnarelli cacio e pepe and a glass of cellar red at the counter for $26.

Kitchen open until 10:30 PM (11 PM Fri–Sat)
