Our Story

A kitchen built on Nonna's recipes and the neighbors who keep coming back.

Trattoria Rosa started as a six-table room with one stove and a very stubborn pasta machine. Nearly four decades later, we still make our tagliatelle, gnocchi, and ragù from scratch, every single day.

We buy from farms and purveyors within a short drive, cook with the seasons, and keep the menu short enough that everything on it gets our full attention.
