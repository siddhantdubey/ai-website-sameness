La Storia · Since 1998

A brownstone dining room built for weeknights and milestones alike.

When Mirella and Marco Conti opened Osteria Vico twenty-eight years ago, they wanted the sort of place they missed from Bologna: unhurried, deeply seasonal, and priced so neighbors could wander in on a Tuesday evening for a bowl of tagliatelle and a quartino of Sangiovese.

Today, their daughter Chiara runs the dining room and cellar while Chef Lorenzo leads the pasta table. Our olive oil still arrives each November in tin cans from Mirella's cousin's grove outside Spoleto, and our cast-iron focaccia still comes out blistered and fragrant at 5:00 sharp.

“Good hospitality isn’t theater. It’s remembering how someone takes their Negroni and making sure the pasta hits the table piping hot.”
— Chiara Conti, Proprietor
