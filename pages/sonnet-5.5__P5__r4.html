Four opinions

We left out more than we put in.

Most tools grow by adding fields. Foreman was built by a general contractor and a former ops lead who kept watching work disappear in the gap between “assigned” and “done.” These are the rules we ended up with.

  1. One owner, always.

    You can add watchers and helpers, but the task has a single name on it. If two people really share it, split it into two tasks. It takes ten seconds and removes the argument later.

  2. No date, no task.

    Foreman won’t save a task without a due date. Not a priority level, not a T-shirt size — a date. “Someday” goes in the notes app.

  3. Handoffs need a yes.

    Passing a task to someone doesn’t move it until they accept. Until then it stays on your list with their name beside it, so nothing falls into a teammate’s inbox and dies there.

  4. Slipping is visible, not shameful.

    Late tasks float to the top of Monday’s view with a plain count of days. You can push a date, but the original stays in the history. People stop padding estimates when they stop being punished for them.

We cut our Tuesday check-in from fifty minutes to eleven. Everyone already knew what was late before they sat down.

Questions

Things people ask before they switch.

Is there a Gantt chart or board view?

There’s a timeline view for projects with dependencies, and a simple column board if you like dragging cards. Neither is the default. The Monday view is.

What if a task really does need two people?

Split it. “Draft the proposal” (Ines) and “Review the proposal” (Colm) are two tasks with two dates, and both of those people now know what they owe.

Can clients or subcontractors use it?

Yes, as guests. They see only tasks they own or are watching, and they never count toward your seat total.

Does it connect to other tools?

Calendar feeds (iCal), email-in to create tasks, and a webhook for anything else. We’d rather do three integrations well than forty badly.

Where does my data live?

On servers in the EU and US, your choice at signup, encrypted at rest. You can export everything at any time, and deletion is permanent after 30 days.
