Three habits, no training required.

Most project tools ask you to learn a system before they help. Stillwork has one board, one list of dates, and one weekly email. You can be useful on the first afternoon.

  1. 01

    Put the work on the board

    Cards are a title, an owner, and a date. Add labels if you want them. Nothing else is required before a card can move.

  2. 02

    Let late work announce itself

    When a date passes without a move, the card turns red and shows up at the top of the board. Nobody has to ask for a status update.

  3. 03

    Read one summary a week

    Friday afternoon you get a single message: what shipped, what slipped, and what's coming up. Reply to it or ignore it.

What we decided not to build.

Every feature in a project tool is a tax on the people using it. These are the ones we kept out on purpose.

No time tracking

If you need hours billed, use a tool built for invoices. Stillwork measures whether work is finished, not how long it took to look busy.

No daily check-ins

The board is the check-in. Asking for a paragraph every morning tends to produce paragraphs, not progress.

Fewer notifications

  • One weekly summary by default
  • Mentions and assignments only
  • Quiet hours that actually stay quiet

Export everything

Boards export to CSV and JSON at any time, including history. Your data is yours to take with you.
