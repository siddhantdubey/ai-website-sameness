How it works

Four opinions, held firmly.

01

Every task has one owner.

Not a group, not "the design team." Kiln won't let you save a task without one name on it. It's the single most useful constraint we know of.

02

Every task has a date.

If you don't know when something is due, say "someday" and it goes in a separate pile that stays out of your way until you promote it.

03

Late is loud. Everything else is quiet.

Overdue work floats to the top in red. There are no badges, no streaks, and no weekly digests about your "productivity score."

04

A list is enough, until it isn't.

Lists are the default. When a project outgrows one, add sections or flip to a timeline. You never have to choose a view on day one.

What Kiln doesn't do

We left these out on purpose.

  • NoCustom fields with 11 types. You get status, owner, date, and notes.
  • NoAutomation builder. Two built-in rules: recurring tasks and "remind me when blocked."
  • NoAI that rewrites your task titles. Your words stay yours.
  • NoPer-seat pricing that punishes you for inviting a contractor.
  • NoTime tracking. Use a tool that's actually good at it; we have an export.
  • NoOnboarding checklist for the onboarding checklist.

Questions

Things people ask before signing up.

Can I get my data out?

Yes, at any time, on any plan. One click gives you a CSV of every task, owner, date, and note. If you cancel, we keep your data for 90 days in case you change your mind, then delete it.

We already use a bigger tool. Why switch?

Maybe don't. If your current tool works and people use it, keep it. Kiln is for teams who've noticed that nobody updates the board anymore.

Does it work on a phone?

The web app is fully usable on mobile. Native iOS and Android apps are in progress; we'll announce them in the changelog, not before.

Who's behind this?

A small independent team. We're funded by customers, not investors, which is why the pricing is boring.
