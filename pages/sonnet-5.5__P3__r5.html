02 — About

I wait for the picture to show up.

I'm Mara — a self-taught photographer who spent a decade chasing weather across coastlines and mountain passes before turning the camera toward people. That patience carries into everything I shoot: I'd rather spend an hour watching the light than ten minutes forcing a pose.

My work has appeared in travel and lifestyle magazines, and I take on a limited number of commissions each year so every project gets real attention.
