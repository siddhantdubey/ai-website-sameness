Our Approach

A small firm on purpose.

We've both worked at large firms. We left to practice the way we think clients deserve: directly, affordably, and without being handed off to someone you've never met.

You get the lawyer.

The attorney who meets you is the one who drafts your documents and answers your calls. No rotating associates.

Price is a promise.

Most matters are flat fee. When something truly can't be, we give you an estimate in writing and tell you before it changes.

Plain language.

Every document comes with a one-page summary of what it does. If you can't explain it to your family, we haven't done our job.

Common Questions

Before you call.

If your question isn't here, ask us. The first conversation is always free.

Is the first consultation really free?

Yes. The first 20-minute call is free and carries no obligation. It's a chance for us to understand your situation and for you to decide whether we're the right fit.

How do your flat fees work?

After the consultation we send a written engagement letter listing exactly what's included and the total price. You pay half to begin and the balance when documents are signed. If the scope changes, we discuss it with you before doing any additional work.

Do you take litigation or criminal cases?

No. We focus on planning and transactional work. If you need a litigator or criminal defense attorney, we're glad to refer you to colleagues we trust.

Can we meet remotely?

Absolutely. Most clients meet with us by video at least once. Signing ceremonies for wills and trusts are usually in person, but we can arrange remote notarization where Oregon law allows it.

What should I bring to the first meeting?

Whatever you have: existing wills, a deed, a draft contract, a list of questions. We'll send a short checklist specific to your matter once you book.
