Three things most plans hide

  1. 01

    What's waiting on what

    Every task names the work it depends on, and Plumb calculates dates from those links. Nobody has to keep forty rows of a spreadsheet in sync by hand.

  2. 02

    How much room is left

    Float is the number of days a task can run late before the launch moves. It's drawn beside every bar, so you can tell a delay that matters from one that doesn't.

    7 days of float
  3. 03

    What you first promised

    The baseline stays on the page as a dashed outline. Six weeks in, you can still see the plan everyone agreed to and how far the real one has drifted.

One email on Monday: what moved, and why.

Plumb compares this week's plan with last week's and writes up the difference. People who never open the app still know where the launch stands.

  • Each change is traced back to the task that caused it.
  • Delays the plan absorbed are listed separately, with the float that's left.
  • If nothing moved, the email is one line long.
