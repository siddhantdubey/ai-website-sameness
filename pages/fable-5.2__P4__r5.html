§ 5Questions

What people ask before they call.

Is the first conversation really free?

Yes. Thirty minutes, with no obligation to hire us. If the matter is simple enough to handle yourself, we will tell you how.

What should I bring?

Any paperwork you already have: a contract, a deed, a letter you received, a prior will. If you have nothing in writing yet, come anyway. A short timeline of what happened and when is the most useful thing you can prepare.

Do you take cases outside Portland?

We work throughout Maine and in New Hampshire, and most meetings can happen by phone or video. Court appearances outside Cumberland and York counties may carry travel time, which we quote in advance.

What if I cannot afford a lawyer?

Ask us about payment plans, which we offer on most flat-fee matters. We also keep a list of legal aid organizations and reduced-fee programs and are glad to point you to the right one.

Is what I tell you confidential?

What you tell us in a consultation is kept confidential, even if you decide not to hire us. Please hold sensitive details for that conversation and leave them out of the contact form below.
