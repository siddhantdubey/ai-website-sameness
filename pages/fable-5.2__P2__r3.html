La nostra storia

Eleven tables and her mother’s ragù.

  • 1989The year Bettina Ferraro signed the lease on a shuttered shoe-repair shop.
  • 38Seats, counting the two stools by the oven that regulars fight over.
  • 6 hrsWhat the ragù takes. We tried five once. Never again.

Bettina came to Marlow Street from Bologna with a pasta board under one arm and no intention of opening a restaurant. Then the neighbors tasted her Sunday cooking, and the matter was more or less decided for her.

She opened with eleven tables, a hand-written menu, and one rule: nothing goes out that she wouldn’t serve at home. The room has grown a little since. The rule hasn’t moved.

Her daughter Carla runs the kitchen now. The pasta is still rolled every morning on the same board, the tomatoes still come from the Aiello farm stand in summer, and Bettina still comes in most Thursdays to tell everyone what they’re doing wrong.

“A good osteria should know your name by the second visit and your order by the third.”
— Bettina Ferraro
