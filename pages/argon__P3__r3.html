THE PRACTICE // BIOGRAPHY

Between structure & solitude.

Working primarily with medium-format film and natural high-latitude light, my practice studies how weathered architecture frames human presence—and how silence registers on silver gelatin and cotton rag paper.

Over the past decade, Søren has collaborated with architectural practices, independent publishing houses, and design studios across Scandinavia, France, and Japan. Each commission is approached as an extended visual essay rather than an isolated campaign.
