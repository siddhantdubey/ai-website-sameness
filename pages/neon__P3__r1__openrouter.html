Biography & Approach

Slow Observation

Studio 4B, Lisbon Ilford Delta 100 · 80mm

“A photograph is less about capturing what stands before the lens, and more about listening to how light alters a room over an hour.”

Born in Lyon and trained in architectural history before turning full-time to photography, my work sits at the intersection of spatial geometry and human quietude. Whether documenting a brutalist pavilion at dawn or a rain-washed Parisian boulevard after midnight, I favor unhurried pacing and natural light.

Most commissions are photographed on medium-format 120 film alongside digital capture for rapid editorial turnaround. Every project is color-graded in-house to preserve delicate shadow detail and tactile tonal transitions.
