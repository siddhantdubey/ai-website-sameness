01 / The Diagnosis

Most trackers reward busywork instead of clarity.

When every minor adjustment triggers a Slack ping and a comment thread disappears into a closed ticket, teams spend Tuesday mornings reconstructing Monday's context.

HABIT 01

Orphaned decisions

Three months after launch, nobody remembers why billing retries happen at 48 hours instead of 24. The answer is buried in a 90-message Slack channel.

Docket fix: Pin a 2-line Decision Note directly to the task ID.
HABIT 02

Unbounded backlogs

A backlog with 420 items isn't a plan; it's a museum of guilt. People stop trusting the list and keep personal sticky notes on their monitors.

Docket fix: Unstarted work auto-archives after 6 weeks unless renewed.
HABIT 03

Perpetual badge anxiety

Real-time red dots turn deep work into customer support. Good software waits until you open it, or sends one calm summary at the end of the week.

Docket fix: Zero unread counters. One Friday 16:00 digest.
02 / Mechanics

Built like an accounting ledger, not a social feed.

Every screen fits on a single mental model. Switch between capacity planning, pinned context, and weekly reporting without configuring twenty custom fields.
