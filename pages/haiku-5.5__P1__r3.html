Features

Everything your team needs. Nothing it doesn't.

Flexible boards

Switch between Kanban, list, and calendar views. Every view stays in sync in real time.

Timelines & dependencies

Map out milestones and link tasks together. Know instantly when a slip will ripple downstream.

Clear ownership

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is there really a free plan?

Yes. The Starter plan is free forever for teams of up to five people, with no credit card required.

Can I import my existing projects?

Tessera imports from CSV and common tools like Trello, Asana, and Jira. Your structure, assignees, and due dates come with you.

How does the 14-day trial work?

Try every Team feature for two weeks. We'll remind you before it ends. If you don't upgrade, your account drops back to the Starter plan and nothing is deleted.

Where is my data stored?

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Enterprise customers can choose data residency in the EU or US.
