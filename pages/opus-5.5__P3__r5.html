About

I photograph the in-between — the pause before something happens.

I'm Ines, a freelance photographer with twelve years behind the camera. I started out shooting film on long hikes through the Carpathians and still approach every job the same way: slowly, patiently, and with a lot of attention to light.

Today I split my time between commissioned editorial work, brand campaigns and personal projects. Whether it's a CEO portrait or a week in the high desert, I aim for images that feel honest and will still hold up in twenty years.
