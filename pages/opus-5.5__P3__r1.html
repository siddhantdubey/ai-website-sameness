02About

I started photographing at fourteen with a borrowed film camera and a habit of waking before sunrise. Neither has changed much.

My work is slow by design: I scout, wait, and return. For portraits, that means time spent talking before any shutter moves. For places, it means learning the light until it does something worth keeping.

I've shot for travel magazines, architecture studios and independent brands, and I still take on a handful of personal commissions each year.
