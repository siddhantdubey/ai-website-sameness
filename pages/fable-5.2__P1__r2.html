A-04 · Questions

Before you move a whole team over.

Can we bring our existing projects in?

Yes. Import from a CSV or a spreadsheet and Plumb maps your columns to tasks, owners and dates. Dependencies come across if your file has them, and you can draw the rest in the timeline.

We run sprints. Does this still fit?

It does. Plan the sprint in the board view and keep the longer roadmap in the timeline. They share the same tasks, so a sprint that runs over shows up against the release date straight away.

What happens when we outgrow the free plan?

Nothing is locked or deleted. You will be asked to upgrade when you add a sixth person or a fourth active project, and archived projects never count towards the limit.

Can we get our data out again?

Any time, on every plan. Export a project or your whole workspace as CSV or JSON, including dependencies and history.
