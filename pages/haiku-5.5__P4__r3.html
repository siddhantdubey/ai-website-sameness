About the Firm

A firm built on direct attention

Harbor & Vale was founded on a simple idea: clients deserve to speak with the attorney handling their case, and they deserve straight answers about cost, timing, and risk.

Our team is intentionally small. That means your matter is never handed off to a junior associate you have never met, and every decision is made by someone who knows your file.

  • Partner-level attention on every matter
  • Transparent fee structures with written estimates
  • Responsive communication, typically within one business day
  • Serving clients across the region, in person and remotely

Our Principles

Honesty before optimism. We will tell you when a case is strong, when it is weak, and what we would do in your position.

Preparation over posturing. Good outcomes come from careful work, not theatrics.

Your interests first. We take on matters we believe we can handle well, and we say so when we cannot.

How We Work

A straightforward path forward

From first call to final resolution, you will always know what happens next.

  1. Consultation

    We listen to your situation, identify the key issues, and tell you honestly whether we can help.

  2. Strategy

    You receive a written plan outlining options, likely timeline, and a clear fee estimate.

  3. Execution

    We handle the work with regular updates, so you are never left guessing where things stand.

  4. Resolution

    We close the matter carefully and make sure you understand what comes next and how to protect it.
