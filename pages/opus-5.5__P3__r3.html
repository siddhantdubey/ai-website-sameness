02 — About

Patient by nature, curious by trade.

I grew up between the Scottish coast and southern Portugal, which is probably why I'm drawn to weather, water and the long minutes before sunset.

For the past twelve years I've worked as an independent photographer, splitting my time between personal landscape projects, editorial assignments and a small number of weddings each year. I shoot on digital and medium-format film, and I edit everything myself.

My approach is unhurried. I'd rather spend an extra hour waiting for the right light than fill a hard drive with near-misses. On commissions, that means careful planning up front, so on the day I can stay out of the way and simply pay attention.
