Can I use Forma with my small team?

Absolutely. The Free plan gives up to three teammates a shared workspace with unlimited tasks, boards, and lists. Move to Team whenever you need more room.

Can we change plans later?

Yes. Upgrade as your team grows, or change your plan at the end of your current billing period.

What’s the difference between boards, lists, and timelines?

Boards organize work by stage. Lists make it easy to scan tasks and priorities. Timelines show how your project fits together over time. They’re different views of the same work.
