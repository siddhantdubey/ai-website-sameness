Questions

Frequently asked

Is the first consultation really free?

Yes. Your initial 30-minute consultation is free and carries no obligation. We'll discuss your situation and whether we're the right fit.

How do you charge for your services?

Depending on the matter, we offer flat fees, capped hourly arrangements, or standard hourly billing. We'll explain the structure and estimate costs in writing before work begins.

Will I work with a partner or an associate?

You'll work directly with a partner on your matter from start to finish.

Do you offer remote meetings?

Yes. We meet by phone, video, or in person at our office, whichever is most convenient for you.
